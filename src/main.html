Hoogwan Lee · M.S. Researcher

Building science and scalable 3D systems through full-stack engineering.

I am an M.S. student in Semiconductor Engineering at SeoulTech and a researcher at the Electronic Systems Laboratory. My research focuses on plasma-enhanced low-temperature hybrid Cu bonding to advance the scalability of 3D integration. My goal is to bridge the gap between precision micro-fabrication and system-level performance, investigating how advanced packaging innovations drive the architectural integrity and reliability of next-generation hardware.

I am deeply interested in 3D integration processes, specifically focusing on the convergence where advanced packaging increasingly adopts precision front-end fabrication methodologies. I am fascinated by the challenge of bridging the gap between micro-scale manufacturing and robust packaging solutions to enable high-density architectures. Furthermore, I am passionate about exploring the holistic system-level impact, dedicated to studying how process-level innovations dictate the performance, reliability, and architectural integrity of the entire hardware ecosystem.
