1. Role and Responsibilities

As a primary manager of S-fab, I was responsible for the end-to-end operational integrity of the cleanroom environment. My role encompassed infrastructure management, equipment installation, and technical instruction, ensuring that the facility met the rigorous demands of both internal and external research projects.

2. Managed Equipment Portfolio

I personally managed and maintained a suite of high-end semiconductor fabrication and characterization tools:

  • Deposition: ICP-CVD (Oxford Instruments)
  • Lithography & Bonding: 8-inch Mask Aligner (EVG 610), Wafer Bonder (EVG 501)
  • Characterization: Shear Test Systems (MFM 1200, DGFT)

3. Operations & Preventive Maintenance (PM)

To maximize tool uptime and process repeatability, I executed comprehensive maintenance protocols:

  • System Upkeep: Conducted routine chamber cleaning, gas supply management, and chiller fluid replenishment.
  • Troubleshooting: Acted as the first responder for equipment malfunctions, diagnosing hardware/software errors to minimize facility downtime.
  • Standardization: Authored and updated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to ensure safe and consistent tool operation for all users.
  • Installation: Managed the initial setup and commissioning of new equipment, ensuring seamless integration into the existing Fab infrastructure.

4. User Training & Technical Consultation

I developed a structured training program to empower researchers and support external industry-academic collaborations:

  • External Process Services: Provided expert consultation and recipe development for external partners, delivering high-quality fabrication results tailored to specific project requirements.
3-Step "Self-User" Certification Program
  1. Phase 1Fundamental Theory

    Comprehensive briefing on equipment mechanisms, safety protocols, and general capabilities.

  2. Phase 2Customized Operation

    Hands-on instruction focused on the user’s specific research applications and experimental goals.

  3. Phase 3Proficiency Evaluation

    Final assessment where I observed the user’s independent operation to verify technical competence and safety compliance before granting access.

Reflections: The Engineer as a System Architect

Managing S-fab was more than just maintaining hardware; it was about architecting a reliable research ecosystem. Through the 3-step training program, I learned that clear communication of technical nuances is just as vital as the engineering itself. Handling diverse external requests also sharpened my ability to quickly adapt established recipes to meet unique, high-precision needs. This experience solidified my capacity to lead a technical facility and mentor the next generation of engineers.
